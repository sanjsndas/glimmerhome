    Glimmer Home Details is an independent journal exploring residential architecture, interior design, materials, and the quiet details that make a house feel like home.

    Founded in 2021 by architectural writer and former interior designer Elena Marchetti, Glimmer Home Details began as a personal notebook — a place to document the ideas, materials, and spatial principles that shape meaningful residential design. Today it is a fully independent publication read by homeowners, designers, architects, and anyone who cares about the built environment.

    We are not a trend site. We do not chase virality or publish sponsored listicles. Instead, we take the long view: studying how light moves through a room, how a material ages, how a floor plan supports daily rituals. Our articles are researched, tested against real projects, and written to be useful for years — not just for a news cycle.

    Every week we publish in-depth guides, case studies, and essays. We interview architects and makers. We visit homes and construction sites. And we distill what we learn into clear, practical knowledge you can apply to your own space — whether you're renovating a single room or designing a house from the ground up.

    Our promise is simple: no fluff, no hidden agendas, no pay-to-play coverage. Just independent design journalism, made with care.

    What Guides Us

    Three principles behind every piece we publish.

    ◻

    Clarity over clutter

    We write to inform, not to impress. Our articles are structured, jargon-light, and grounded in practical application. If a concept can't be explained simply, we work harder until it can.

    ◈

    Honesty in materials

    We celebrate surfaces that age gracefully — concrete, timber, stone, linen. We believe good design respects the nature of materials rather than masking them with synthetic imitations.

    ◉

    Independence, always

    We accept no sponsored content, no affiliate commissions, and no brand payments. Our recommendations come from research and experience, not from advertising deals.

    Editorial Policy

    Glimmer Home Details is committed to independent, high-quality design journalism. Our editorial decisions are made solely by our editors — never by advertisers, brands, or external partners.

    • We do not publish sponsored articles, native advertising, or paid placements.
    • We do not use affiliate links. If we mention a product or material, it is because it is relevant to the story — not because we earn a commission.
    • We fact-check all technical claims and credit sources where appropriate.
    • We correct errors promptly and transparently.
    • Our team has no financial relationships with the brands or firms we cover, unless explicitly disclosed in the article.

    If you have questions about our editorial standards, please contact us.
