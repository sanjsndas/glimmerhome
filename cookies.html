    • Cookies Policy

    Last updated: 1 March 2025. This policy explains how we use cookies and how you can control them.

    Glimmer Home Details ("we", "us", or "our") operates the website www.glimmerhomedetails.com (the "Site"). This Cookies Policy explains what cookies are, how we use them, and your choices regarding their use. It should be read alongside our Privacy Policy.

    In short: We use a small number of essential and analytical cookies to make the Site work properly and understand how it's used. You can manage or disable cookies through your browser at any time.

    1. What Are Cookies?

    Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, as well as to provide information to the site owners. Cookies do not typically contain any information that personally identifies you, but personal information that we store about you may be linked to the information stored in and obtained from cookies.

    2. How We Use Cookies

    We use cookies for the following purposes:

    • Essential cookies: These are necessary for the Site to function properly. They enable core features such as security, network management, and accessibility. You cannot opt out of these cookies without severely affecting the Site's functionality.
    • Analytical / performance cookies: These allow us to recognise and count the number of visitors and see how visitors move around the Site. This helps us improve the way the Site works, for example, by ensuring users find what they are looking for easily.
    • Functionality cookies: These are used to recognise you when you return to the Site. This enables us to personalise content for you, greet you by name, and remember your preferences (for example, your choice of language or region).

    3. Specific Cookies We Use

    Below is a detailed list of the cookies we currently use on the Site. We may update this list from time to time as our Site evolves.

    Cookie Name Type Purpose Duration
    glimmer_session Essential Maintains your session state across page requests. Session
    glimmer_consent Essential Stores your cookie consent preferences. 1 year
    _ga Analytics Used by Google Analytics to distinguish unique users and track site usage. 2 years
    _gid Analytics Used by Google Analytics to distinguish users (short-term). 24 hours
    _gat Analytics Used by Google Analytics to throttle request rate. 1 minute

    3.1 Third-Party Cookies

    Some cookies are placed by third-party services that appear on our pages. We do not control these cookies. The third parties we currently use include:

    • Google Analytics: For traffic analysis and understanding user behaviour. You can learn more about Google's privacy practices at policies.google.com/privacy.
    • Embedded content: Occasionally we embed content from platforms like Instagram or Pinterest. These platforms may set their own cookies when you interact with the embedded content.

    4. Your Cookie Choices

    You have several options to manage or disable cookies:

    • Browser settings: Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can set your browser to refuse cookies or delete certain cookies. The method varies by browser; check your browser's "Help" or "Settings" menu for instructions.
    • Opt-out of analytics: You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.
    • Do Not Track: Some browsers have a "Do Not Track" feature. We do not currently respond to Do Not Track signals, but you can still manage cookies as described above.

    Please note that if you disable or refuse cookies, some parts of the Site may become inaccessible or not function properly. Essential cookies are required for basic site operations.

    5. Changes to This Cookies Policy

    We may update this Cookies Policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology, legislation, or our practices. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically to stay informed about our use of cookies.
